Auto-submit

Auto-submit is an account setting: turn it on, set its daily limit and save your screening answers in CareerCraft → Settings → Applying. It is off by default, never applies on LinkedIn or Naukri, and even when on the extension stops for sign-in, account creation, verification codes, CAPTCHAs, unanswered or AI-drafted answers, validation errors and consent or legal checkboxes.
